# Pharos gap graph demonstration

[Watch the 79-second captioned video](https://github.com/iandriver/pharos-gap-kg/releases/download/submission-2026-11-17-r3/pharos-gap-kg-gaps-demo.mp4) or [open the Parkinson disease report](https://pm4ie6tprh.us-east-1.awsapprunner.com/?id=MONDO%3A0005180&name=Parkinson%20disease&kind=trait&view=gaps). The video has no voice track. The November 17 r2 import video used the macOS Samantha synthetic voice.

| Time | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:11 | Parkinson disease has 345 tied genes. For 97, the release holds other evidence but no GWAS link. |
| 0:11–0:21 | The live page lists TMEM175, the missing compound evidence and the 1 micromolar potency cutoff that would close its gap. |
| 0:21–0:33 | At a common variant, detecting an odds ratio of 1.05 would take about 55,000 cases at the study’s control-to-case ratio. |
| 0:33–0:46 | A dated Alzheimer’s study named ABI3, APP and PLCG2 among the 74 genes with other evidence and no release GWAS link. The local update and the public baseline are identified on screen. |
| 0:46–0:57 | A recorded study import shows a rejected mapping. The scientist can inspect the reason and preview the revised mapping. |
| 0:57–1:09 | An Alzheimer’s CSF protein panel shows why 264 matching symbols produced 285 proposed target records. The mappings stayed at preview. |
| 1:09–1:19 | On links reported after 2018, context raised LightGCN’s mean average precision from 0.0825 to 0.0866. A simpler neighbour method scored 0.0969. |

For a live presentation, switch from Parkinson disease to another covered disease and click “The graph behind this answer (KGX)” to export its evidence. The Alzheimer's update is demonstrated in the [study record](../findings/AD_STUDY.md#added-29-oct-2026-the-study-as-a-dated-source).
