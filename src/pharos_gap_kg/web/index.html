What is known about a gene or a disease, and what is missing

Look up a disease, a human gene or a drug. The answers come from NIH Pharos 3.19: GWAS links between genes and traits, each dated by its first study (2005 to 2023), and what nine disease sources, drug indications and expression studies say. Each answer names its sources and its limits. No language model writes or checks them.

Try:

Is a gene linked to a disease, and on what evidence?

With no link, the answer says what nearby evidence exists, where the gene ranks among the disease's candidates, and what size of study an absent link calls for.

How to read an answer

A GWAS link places an associated variant at a gene. It does not say the gene causes the disease. "No link" means no study in the source had reported one by that year; it is not evidence of no association, and the answer gives the size of effect the largest study could have seen. A gap is evidence the graph does not hold, with what would close it and how often such gaps have closed since.

Ask from Claude or another assistant

The same questions are served over MCP at /mcp. In Claude, add that address as a custom connector (Settings, Connectors) and ask, for example, "Using pharos-gap-kg, what are the gaps for Parkinson disease?". Every answer on this page is also at /api/<question> as JSON.
